Flux: Brand Identity & Campaign

A bold identity for spaces designed around creativity and connection.

Flux is a conceptual coworking brand built around the idea that our surroundings can influence how we create, work, and connect. Vibrant color, oversized typography, organic forms, and architectural photography create an energetic identity that moves across digital and physical spaces.

Tandem: Brand Identity & Visual System

A warm, human-centered identity for clearer communication between educators and families.

Tandem helps teachers turn everyday classroom moments into clear, warm notes for families. The identity is built on two sides coming together: overlapping shapes, soft color, and small hand-drawn touches that keep an AI product feeling human. It carries through the app, from suggestions to tone controls, always leaving the teacher in charge of the final word.
